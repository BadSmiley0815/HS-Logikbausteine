14784 – Message store (v1.1)

Block ID 14784Version 1.1HSL 3.0

Own message archive on the HomeServer, independent of the Experte message archives. One instance = one archive. Up to 16 signals with text ON text;OFF text;level ON;level OFF (0 info, 1 warning, 2 alarm), a free-text input, acknowledge and clear. Entries survive restarts. Served by visu server 14770 (from v1.11) at api/messages; acknowledge via POST api/messages/ack.

From v1.1: I42 = 1 records signals and free text only while I43 or I44 = 1 (e.g. alarm armed) plus I45 seconds afterwards – for intrusion tracking. Up to 12 sources (I49–I60) with names (I61–I72): if a signal (I47 list, empty = all) changes within I46 seconds after a source telegram, “ – name” is appended to the message; otherwise the text from I48.
