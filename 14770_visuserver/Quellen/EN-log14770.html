Interactive chart in the Gira visu

  1. Insert 14770 (I1 visu-daten, I2 8092) and one 14771 per value (I6 unique ID, I7 name, I8 unit, I5 = O1 of 14770).
  2. Text element in chart size, static text:
    @iframe=/visu-daten/chart?id=room_act,room_set,room_valve&u=i&title=Room
    (no quotes, spaces as %20)
  3. Transfer, then open the visu with extras=1:
    https://<HS>/hsvisu/pages/V1?user=<user>&pw=<password>&cl=<design>&extras=1
    extras=1 (undocumented Gira visu option) enables @iframe= and @link=URL,Text in text elements. It disappears from the address bar after login but stays active until the page is reloaded.

Drag with the mouse to zoom, double-click or “Zoom zurück” to reset. Click a legend entry to hide/show a series, periods 1 h … 3 years (years with slider), second unit on right axis. Parameters: id, u, n, title, refresh, nav=0, hide, ymin, ymax, color, key.

3-year view with slider

Fallback: chart as image

/visu-daten/chart.svg?id=…&w=940&h=420 as CSS background of a text element: background:url(/visu-daten/chart.svg?id=…) no-repeat center/100% 100%; – static, loaded with the page. view=table = value table, chart.png = PNG.
